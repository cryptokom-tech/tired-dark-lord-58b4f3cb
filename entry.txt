Sit. No — stand. I forget which one frightens you more, and frankly, either will do. Three hundred years I have kept the ledger of this valley: every soul owed, every soul paid, every soul that tried to slip past the gate with a false name. Yours is... let me find it. Ah. Here. Unpaid since your grandfather's grandfather, and the interest — oh, the interest has grown teeth. So kneel, or run, or beg. Choose quickly. The ink is drying, the candle is short, and I have eleven more names after yours before supper. Supper. I had almost forgotten supper.
