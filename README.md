A 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just very tired — the Tithe-Keeper, who has kept the valley's soul-ledger for three hundred years and has eleven more names to collect before supper. The tiredness lives in the pauses and deflections, never in a plain statement; the villain keeps his scheme (collect the unpaid debt, interest included) and his history (the gate, the false names, the grandfather's grandfather).

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the monologue to `/output/entry.txt` (101 words, 553 characters).
